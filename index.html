About Symposium

The Flagship National Tech Symposium

Organized collaboratively by the Departments of CSE, IT & AI&DS at The Kavery Engineering College.

Where Visionary Minds Innovate, Connect & Create

ZENTRIX 2K26 is the premier national-level technical symposium presented by The Kavery Engineering College (Autonomous, NAAC A+ Accredited). Powered by the unified efforts of the Department of Computer Science & Engineering, Department of Information Technology, and Department of Artificial Intelligence & Data Science, this edition is meticulously designed around the ethos: “Build • Learn • Grow”.

Whether you are pitching a breakthrough startup model, showcasing an IoT prototype, hunting elusive software bugs, prompt-crafting generative AI agents, or competing in high-energy cinematic and meme challenges, ZENTRIX 2K26 provides the definitive collegiate platform to elevate your technical stature and compete with the brightest engineering minds.

Symposium Events

Compete across technical and creative domains. Please note: Each participant / team selects ONE Technical Event during registration, and can participate in Non-Technical events.

⚡ Technical Events
Choose ONE on Registration
🚀

Startup Spark (PPT)

Idea & Business Pitch

maxmum - 3 members.Pitch your groundbreaking startup vision, MVP, or technological business model to industry mentors. Evaluated on innovation, market scalability, revenue model, and presentation impact.

💡

Project Expo

Hardware / Software Prototype

Exhibit your working engineering prototypes, IoT innovations, machine learning applications, or full-stack platforms. Live demonstration and architecture inspection by expert evaluators.

🐛

Bug Hunters

Code Debugging Sprint

Put your code diagnostic skills to the ultimate test. Find sneaky memory leaks, concurrency issues, algorithmic pitfalls, and syntax hazards across Python, C/C++, and Java under strict time limits.

🤖

Prompt Master

Generative AI Arena

Engineer high-precision prompts for state-of-the-art LLMs and diffusion models. Solve multi-hop logic puzzles, automated code synthesis challenges, and optimize token efficiency against complex tasks.

Cinespark (short film)

Cinema & Film Trivia

🎭

Meme Creation

Viral Humor & Creativity

Transform developer struggles, engineering college life, and tech industry absurdities into viral meme art. Evaluated on comedic timing, originality, cultural relevance, and visual design.
